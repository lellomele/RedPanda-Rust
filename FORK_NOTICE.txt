English
The initial reference is upstream v3.4 (2fff3c2584e99ccd5aafdf897120e00e9c036515).
Implementation used the later upstream checkout cbe084d162987fb90867b12f279af94c7a1d6a38
(3.5.0-beta.2). Upstream changes between these revisions are separate from the
fork additions. See docs/FORK_CHANGES.md and docs/FEATURES.md.

The fork adds Italian localization and local GCC diagnostic translation,
coordinated themes and coloured icons, richer snippets and searchable preferences,
selective artifact cleanup, stronger persistence/recovery and Windows packaging.
The documentation explains the applicable limits; not every fault is recoverable.
